How to Crack IIT JEE Main 2025: A Complete Study Plan

A step-by-step study plan to crack IIT JEE Main 2025 with the right strategy, mock tests, and time management tips.

IIT JEE Main is one of the most competitive engineering entrance exams in India. Every year, over 10 lakh students appear for JEE Main but only a fraction make it to the top ranks. With the right strategy and consistent practice, cracking JEE Main is absolutely achievable.

Understanding the JEE Main Exam Pattern

JEE Main consists of two papers:

  • Paper 1 – For B.E./B.Tech (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • Paper 2 – For B.Arch and B.Planning

Paper 1 has 90 questions in total — 30 each from Physics, Chemistry, and Maths. The exam is 3 hours long.

12-Month Study Plan for JEE Main

Months 1–4: Build Your Foundation

Start with NCERT textbooks for all three subjects. Don't skip NCERT — JEE Main directly tests NCERT concepts, especially in Chemistry. Make short notes as you go.

Months 5–8: Strengthen Concepts

Move to reference books:

  • Physics: H.C. Verma, DC Pandey
  • Chemistry: O.P. Tandon, NCERT (Physical), VK Jaiswal (Inorganic)
  • Mathematics: R.D. Sharma, Cengage, Arihant

Months 9–11: Practice and Mock Tests

This is the most critical phase. Take at least 2 full-length mock tests per week. Analyze every mistake. Track which chapters you are weakest in and revise them.

Month 12: Revision and Final Prep

Stop learning new topics. Focus only on revision, formula sheets, and mock tests. Attempt previous year papers from the last 10 years.

Key Tips to Crack JEE Main

1. Master the basics first

Most students make the mistake of jumping to advanced problems without understanding fundamentals. Spend at least 60% of your time on basics.

2. Time management during the exam

Do not spend more than 2 minutes on any question. If stuck, mark it and move on. Return to it later.

3. Attempt mock tests regularly

Mock tests are the single most effective way to prepare. They simulate real exam conditions, improve speed, and help identify weak areas. On Dawnmentor, you can take unlimited chapter-wise and full-length mock tests.

4. Focus on high-weightage chapters

For Physics: Mechanics, Electrostatics, Modern Physics

For Chemistry: Organic Chemistry, Chemical Bonding, Equilibrium

For Maths: Calculus, Algebra, Coordinate Geometry

5. Avoid negative marking traps

JEE Main has negative marking of -1 for wrong answers in MCQ sections. Only attempt questions you are at least 70% sure about.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring NCERT Chemistry
  • Not practicing enough numerical problems in Physics
  • Skipping revision of completed topics
  • Studying without a timetable

Conclusion

Cracking IIT JEE Main requires consistent effort, smart strategy, and regular practice. Start early, follow a structured plan, and take mock tests regularly to track your progress. With dedication and the right resources, your IIT dream is within reach.
