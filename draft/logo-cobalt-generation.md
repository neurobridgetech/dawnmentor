# Cobalt logo

Built-in image generation. Final direction: a flat cobalt and navy orbital Ln Physics identity for the permanent cobalt theme. Earlier glossy assets are retained as source history.

## Final prompt

Create a minimalist flat vector-style education brand logo, text exactly 'Ln Physics'. A compact cobalt-blue Ln monogram on the left encircled by a single thin tilted elliptical orbit with one tiny solid electron dot. To its right the word Physics in clean bold geometric dark navy sans serif. Small flat atom symbol above the right shoulder of the n, drawn with two intersecting ellipses and a dot. One single horizontal lockup. Solid ink shapes in cobalt #1f4b99 and navy #0c1325. White negative space. Absolutely no glow, gradients, shadows, textures, 3D effects or background. Crisp sharply defined boundaries like an SVG exported to PNG. Transparent background. All letters completely opaque and highly legible. Generous spacing between monogram and word Physics. Horizontal 3:1 composition with padding around entire design. High resolution 2400px.
