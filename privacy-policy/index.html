How this LN Physics website handles information.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This website

This website presents information about QuantEdge by LN Physics, teachers, future learning resources and blog articles. It does not provide local account registration, collect passwords or accept answer-sheet uploads.

Browsing and search

Article search and category filters run in your browser. This version does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Fonts and images are served with the website. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the site.

Contacting us

Email links open your email application. If you choose to send a message, the information you include is sent through your email provider to Er.sanketvyas@gmail.com so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

External platforms

Chanakya AI and Google Maps are separate services. Following a link takes you to that service, where its own privacy practices apply. LN Physics does not receive your Chanakya AI password through this website.

Questions about your information

Contact Er.sanketvyas@gmail.com with questions or requests about information you have shared with us.
